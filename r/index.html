The problem

The market rewards discipline.
Most investors act on emotion.

When to buy and sell?

Wait for a dip and it never comes. Buy near the top, sell in the panic. Timing is where most investors lose.

Market corrections are brutal

QQQ lost 83% from 2000 to 2002, more than half in 2008, and a third in 2022. Buy and hold means sitting through all of it.

No process, no edge

Most investors act on headlines, tips and gut feel, with no fixed rules for when to get in or out. Without a process, every decision is a guess.

The solution

Diversified strategies.
Targeted risk.

Three strategies analyze the market in different ways, drawing on over 50 signals between them. Together they build a view on where the market is heading, and a dynamic risk framework turns that view into one number each day: the optimal exposure to QQQ.

The health check

Long-term

Gauges the health of the market as a whole: how broad the buying is, how investors are positioned and how much stress is building. While the market is robust, it holds a long bias, often for a year or more, and steps aside when it turns fragile.

The dip buyer

Tactical

Looks for moments of overreaction, when investors sell in a panic and prices fall further than they should. Drawing on dozens of independent signals, it scales in as others capitulate and takes profits as the market recovers.

The short side

Short

Steps in when the market is weak and stress is high. Rather than just sitting out the decline, it goes short, up to 100%, so falling markets become an opportunity rather than something to fear.

FAQ

Common questions

Can it lose money?

Yes. No model gets every call right, and nobody knows what the future holds. There will be losing trades and periods when it falls in value.

What does it cost?

The daily email is free.

Is this investment advice?

No. It is the published output of a model, the same for everyone. It does not know your circumstances, and what you do with it is up to you.

Do I need margin?

For part of it. About a third of the time the position is above 100%, up to 150%, which means borrowing. Short positions, about one day in six, need a margin account. Without either, you would hold at most 100% and sit on the sidelines instead of going short, which is not the same model.

What is the long-only version?

The same model with every short signal replaced by cash, for accounts that cannot sell short. It keeps the long positions, including those above 100%. Use the Long/short and Long only switch on any chart to compare them.
