How it works

Small edges, stacked.

The model doesn’t try to call every move. It stacks lots of small odds in its favor, and pushes hardest when all the evidence points the same way.

The health check

Long-term

The long-term strategy looks past the price of QQQ to the internal health of the market as a whole: is it robust or fragile? A robust market has broad participation, with stocks rising together. A fragile one is held up by fewer and fewer stocks, with stress building underneath.

It reads this using nine signals, each looking at a different aspect of investor positioning and behavior using price, volume, sentiment, seasonality, volatility. Together they build conviction about where the market is heading over the longer run.

It changes rarely. It is either long or flat, never short, and a single long-term position can last a year or more.

Why never short? Bear markets are where the biggest up days happen, so a long-term short is a dangerous thing to hold. Instead it steps aside and leaves the downside to the short side strategy, which trades on a much shorter, day-to-day horizon.

The dip buyer

Tactical

Within any market, short, sharp pullbacks often reverse within days. Fear and hurried selling can push prices further than the news deserves, and the snap-back tends to come quickly. The tactical strategy looks for these moments using dozens of signals across a wide array of data and factors, including seasonality, relative performance, momentum, velocity, trend, volume, volatility and sentiment.

No single signal decides. The more of them line up, the more it buys, so the position grows with the weight of evidence rather than on any one reading. It sells quickly, looking to de-risk as soon as the bounce comes, so it is rarely in a trade for long.

It is also aware of market conditions. In calmer markets a smaller setup can be enough; in riskier markets it needs more conviction before it steps in, and it holds back when the evidence is mixed.

When conviction is at its highest, it will take selective leverage and position up to 150% long.

The short side

Short side

This strategy spends much of its time waiting. It stays on the sidelines until the long-term strategy signals that the market has turned fragile and vulnerable.

Then it steps in. It looks for moments of unfounded optimism and overconfidence, when other investors are still buying into a weak market, and fades that buying by going short, up to 100% of the account.

Like the tactical strategy, it aims to get out of each trade as soon as it can, keeping risk and time in the market to a minimum.

Together, the tactical and short side strategies let the model profit from the big moves in both directions that come with crashes and long bear markets.

Risk framework

Strategy and portfolio limits

The long-term and tactical strategies are summed and capped at 150% long, and the short side strategy is netted against them. The result is the day's net exposure to QQQ, as a percentage of account equity, from −100% to +150%.

Why three strategies

Different edges for different markets

Each strategy has its own edge, so each makes its money at different times and in different ways. Combined, they allow the model to profit whether markets are rising or falling.

Execution

Set at the close, every day

The model follows the same routine every day. The position is calculated and traded at the close, then checked again that evening once the final market data are in.

Step 1

The position

At the close, the model gathers the day's new market data, runs all three strategies and nets them into one target exposure to QQQ.

Step 2

Published

The target exposure, and any change from the day before, is published on this site and emailed to subscribers within seconds of the close. Every day is kept in the archive and timestamped in a public Git repository.

Step 3

The final check

Some market data arrive later in the evening, so the model runs again on the final numbers. A change is rare, on average once or twice a year. If the position does change, an update is published and emailed straight away.
