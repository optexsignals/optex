Welcome to the OPTEX Market Timing Model.

Thanks for following along. You'll now get the OPTEX QQQ position direct to your inbox every day.

What you'll receive

  • The position: long, short or on the sidelines, as a percentage of the account.
  • The change at the close: from the old position to the new one, if anything changed. Days with a change are marked 🚨 TRADE ALERT in the subject line.
  • The last 15 days: each day's position, and how the long-term, tactical and short strategies make it up.
  • Performance: the model against QQQ for the day, the month and the year to date, with a chart of the last 12 months.

When it arrives

At the close, 4pm New York time. Some data arrive later in the evening, so the model runs again on the final numbers. It is rare for this to change the position, on average once or twice a year; when it does, an update is emailed straight away. There is no email when the US market is closed.

Using Gmail? The first email may land in the Promotions tab. Drag it to Primary and choose “Yes” when Gmail asks, and every email after that arrives in your main inbox.

Following it

  • The position is a percentage of the account, so it works at any size. With a $100,000 account, a position of +120% is $120,000 of QQQ.
  • Every trade happens at the close. Nothing changes during the day, and there are no stop-losses.
  • Above 100% and short positions need a margin account. Without one you would hold at most 100% and sit on the sidelines instead of going short, which is not the same model.
  • Following the long-only version? Simply ignore negative positions and hold cash instead. A short position of −50%, for example, means 0% in QQQ for you.
