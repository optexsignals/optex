What you'll receive

One email each trading day, with the portfolio for that day's open:

  • Allocation: how much of the portfolio is in each of the three strategies, and the net exposure to the market.
  • Changes at the open: the stocks to sell and buy, and any change to the futures position.
  • Every position: each stock and its size, as a percentage of the account.
  • Performance: the portfolio against the S&P 500 for the day, the month and the year to date.

When it arrives

The models run after the US market closes at 4pm New York time. The email goes out in the early hours, New York time, usually by about 1am ET (6am in London), many hours before the 9:30am ET open.

Friday's email arrives early on Saturday morning and covers Monday's open. There is no email when the US market is closed.

How the portfolio works

Three strategies, each with its own edge, run as one portfolio:

  • Core owns strong, leading stocks that have settled into a calm, steady advance, and holds them until volatility returns. Each position is 1.5% of the portfolio, with up to 50 holdings across sectors.
  • Contrarian buys great companies when panic selling drives them far below fair value, and sells as soon as they snap back, usually within days. Each purchase is 3.3% of the portfolio, up to 10% in any one stock. Most of the time it waits in cash.
  • Compass reads the whole market every day and uses Nasdaq-100 index futures to set the portfolio's net exposure: close to zero on most days, long or short when it has high conviction. Net exposure always stays between −100% and +100%.

Stocks are never bought on margin, and Contrarian has first call on cash: when it needs room, Core's oldest positions are sold, and bought back later if they still qualify.

Following it

  • Sizes are percentages of the account, so they work at any size. With a $100,000 account, a 1.5% position is $1,500.
  • Every trade happens at the open. Nothing changes during the day, and there are no stop-losses: each position is closed by its strategy's own rules.
  • Compass uses Nasdaq-100 futures. Without them, you would hold the stocks with their full market risk, which is not the same portfolio. Futures contracts come in fixed sizes, so smaller accounts can only match the futures position approximately.

Nothing hidden
