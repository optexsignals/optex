Factor exposure

Alpha beyond factors.

80% of the portfolio's return is alpha. The rest comes from factor exposures that balance one another across the three strategies.

Core

Behaves like a low-volatility trend portfolio, with a market beta of 0.4. On top of its factor exposures it adds 6% a year of alpha.

Contrarian

Its main factor exposure is short-term reversal, as expected of a strategy paid to provide liquidity. Beyond that it adds 3% a year of alpha.

Compass

The largest source of alpha, at 15% a year. Its returns come from timing the market each day, not from holding factor exposures.

FAQ

Common questions

Is leverage used?

Stock positions are fully funded, with no margin borrowing. Net exposure stays between −100% and +100%. Gross exposure reaches 300% only when Compass takes the portfolio fully net short while the stock strategies are fully invested.

Does it scale?

The strategy is built to scale. Every stock position is a large-cap US name with deep daily liquidity, no position exceeds 10% of capital, and the index futures are among the most liquid contracts in the world.

Is this investment advice?

No. It is the published output of a model, the same for everyone. It does not know your circumstances, and what you do with it is up to you.
